The idea

A native macOS music player

Rift is pure player. Liquid Glass design, a Dynamic Island in your notch, and your data stays on your Mac. No cloud, no telemetry — pure privacy.

Connect your Google account to unlock YouTube Music's full library and recommendation engine. Everything flows into native SwiftUI: listen, download, build playlists, track your stats. The architecture goes further — each music service plugs in behind the same playback interface, so one library, one player, many platforms arrives without changing the UI.

Notch

Lives in your notch

The MacBook notch becomes a Dynamic Island — album art and a dancing equalizer peek out while music plays; hover and it blooms into a full glass transport: shuffle, seek, repeat, like.

Native

Feels like macOS

Liquid Glass surfaces, real vibrancy, native menus, media keys, Control Center, and a menu-bar mini player with live track title. Built like macOS because it's part of macOS.

Private

Keeps your data yours

Listening history, stats, likes and playlists stored on your Mac. No telemetry, no analytics, no cloud middleman. Credentials live in Keychain, nowhere else.

From source

Clone, open the Xcode project, build. Xcode 26+ · macOS 26 Tahoe.

A notch Mac is needed for the Dynamic Island; everything else works on any Mac running Tahoe. Streaming uses yt-dlp under the hood — Homebrew if available, Python 3.10+ fallback, or a standalone binary otherwise.

The core rule: Nothing above PlaybackSource knows where audio comes from. That seam turns "a YouTube Music client" into "a player for everything" — each new service is a new conformer, the same UI never changes.
